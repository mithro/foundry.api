# The PCB long-tail evidence, with every source

**A standalone, checkable summary.** Everything here comes from audited filings and IPO
prospectuses lodged with stock exchanges. Every document is linked. Every derived number shows its
arithmetic. **You should be able to disagree with our reading without taking anything on trust.**

*Compiled 2026-09-26 from [`demand/long-tail-businesses.md`](demand/long-tail-businesses.md) (`SMB-1`),
[`demand/pcb-industry-comparables.md`](demand/pcb-industry-comparables.md) (`PCB-1`…`PCB-14`) and
[`analyses/long-tail-pays-for-the-capital.md`](analyses/long-tail-pays-for-the-capital.md). Those
files carry the full entries, the caveats and the working; this one is the index.*

---

## Why this evidence exists at all

**China's listing rules require a margin breakdown by order size and sales channel.** No US 10-K,
UK annual report or German *Jahresabschluss* carries that table. That regulatory accident is the
only reason any of the following is public, and it is why the finding could be **replicated across
independent filings with different sponsors and different auditors** rather than resting on one
company's candour.

**Read the caution in "How to check this yourself" before quoting anything below.**

## 1. The primary document: JLC's IPO prospectus

**深圳嘉立创科技集团股份有限公司** — Shenzhen JLC Technology Group Co., Ltd., parent of **JLCPCB**
and **EasyEDA**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Document | 招股说明书（申报稿） — IPO prospectus, **filed draft** |
| Filed with | Shenzhen Stock Exchange, **April 2026** |
| Auditor | 容诚会计师事务所 (Rongcheng CPA), report **容诚审字[2026]518Z0073** |
| Auditor's network | RSM International's China member firm, trading as *RSM China CPA LLP* ([rsm.global/china](https://www.rsm.global/china)) |
| Reporting period | FY2023, FY2024, FY2025 |
| Units | 万元 (*wàn yuán*), i.e. CNY 10,000 |

**📄 [The prospectus PDF, from the exchange's own document server](http://reportdocs.static.szse.cn/UpFiles/rasinfodisc1/202604/RAS_202604_22164565DA37A2A6A74520902DB3E1B3350542.pdf)**

### What JLC says about itself, in its own words

Its glossary defines the term:

> 长尾需求 指 对需求量低但种类很多的产品或服务的需求
>
> *"Long-tail demand: demand for products or services where the quantity demanded is low but the
> variety is very large."*

And it says serving that demand **is** the business:

> 公司主要面向客户"多品类、小批量、高频次"的长尾需求
>
> *"The Company mainly addresses customers' long-tail demand for 'many varieties, small batches,
> high frequency'."*

### The numbers

| | |
|---|---:|
| Paying users | **1,358,700** |
| Orders per year | **21.3 million** |
| Average order | **~US$67** |
| Net margin | **12.65%** |
| Top five customers | **1.16% of revenue** |
| Largest single customer | **0.28% of revenue** |

**Gross margin, split by order size and channel — the table that matters:**

| Segment | Gross margin |
|---|---:|
| Sample and small batch | **36.24%** |
| Medium and large batch | **2.76%** |
| Offline big-batch channel | **−6.71%** (loss-making) |

⚠️ **28.06% is the blended whole-PCB figure and must not be quoted as the small-batch margin** — it
already contains the 2.76% segment. This is the single most common way to misread the table.

### What we derived from it, with the arithmetic

The long tail is **75.57% of PCB revenue** but **97.60% of gross profit**. From which:

- **Big-batch work must turn over 13.1× the revenue to service the same capital.**
- Holding the cost structure fixed, a big-batch-only JLC implies a **−12.74% net margin**.

Full working: **[`analyses/long-tail-pays-for-the-capital.md`](analyses/long-tail-pays-for-the-capital.md)**

### JLC owns its plant, and its own impairment is the best test

Five production bases on about a million square metres; **CNY 3,257,464,600** of net fixed assets
including **CNY 952,981,900** of buildings; **CNY 1.395bn** of capex in 2025; registered land titles.

And it took a **CNY 131,365,100 fixed-asset impairment** because:

> PCB 中大批量订单相对不饱和
>
> *"medium- and large-batch PCB orders are relatively under-full"*

**The big-batch equipment sat idle while the long-tail plant ran at 76.78% utilisation.** The
company wrote down the volume factories, not the long-tail ones. *(`PCB-2`)*

## 2. The replications — independent filings, different sponsors

**This is the part that makes the finding more than one company's story.**

| Company | Ticker | What it prints | Document |
|---|---|---|---|
| **强达电路 Qiangda** | SZSE **301628** | Gross margin by **order area**: **44.86%** under 5 m² against **7.00%** over 50 m², across four periods from the same two factories. Tail = 85.79% of revenue, **98.40% of gross profit** (JLC: 97.60%) | [IPO prospectus, 2024-10-17](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-10-17/1221409205.PDF) |
| **金百泽 Jinbaize** | SZSE **301041** | The same cut three years earlier (2018–2020) — **plus the disclosure nobody else makes: customer concentration by order size inside one company**, rising **28.50% → 52.25%** as orders get bigger | [IPO prospectus, 2021-08-04](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2021-08-04/1210652347.PDF) |
| **中富电路 Zhongfu** | SZSE **300814** | **The controlled test.** A *large-batch* house that also runs small batch, ordered by the exchange to split margin by batch. **Small batch wins all eight cells**, across two product types and four periods | [Exchange reply, 2023-07-18](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2023-07-18/1217324419.PDF) |
| **本川智能 Benchuan** | SZSE **300964** | Splits by product family and layer count instead: the boards it says are "mainly small-batch or sample" carry **53.98%** against **18.26%** for the rest | [Annual report, 2026-02-26](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-02-26/1224985712.PDF) |
| **崇达技术 Chongda** | SZSE **002815** | Told the exchange in 2016 that sample > small batch > large batch is **a property of the industry**, and gave the mechanism: a sample house must leave equipment idle and charges for it | [Exchange reply, 2016-09-21](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2016-09-21/1202711273.PDF) |

### The counter-case, from the same disclosure regime

**迅捷兴 Xunjiexing (SSE 688655) ran the experiment forwards and lost.** A declared sample-board
specialist with "over ten thousand" customers:

- Revenue **+42.47%**, gross profit **−15.37%** — because the growth was all large batch
- Large-batch revenue nearly doubled (**+77.93%**) while large-batch gross profit fell **44%**
- Four customers at **32.03% of revenue**, sold at a combined **−11.26% gross margin**
- Net loss CNY 22.4m; utilisation 41.55%; the new Zhuhai plant at **−44.45%**
- Ordered by the Shanghai exchange to break out FY2025 margin by order size: sample boards are
  **23.37% of revenue** and **83.50% of gross profit**

📄 [FY2025 exchange-ordered breakdown, 2026-05-12](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-05-12/1225292252.PDF) ·
[Annual report 2026-03-28](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-28/1225048380.PDF) ·
[2025-03-18](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-03-18/1222822088.PDF) ·
[2024-04-27](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-04-27/1219887245.PDF)

**This matters: a long tail is necessary, not sufficient.** The same company is a declared sample
specialist with ten thousand customers and it is losing money.

**And customer count predicts neither margin nor concentration.** Qiangda has ~3,000 customers,
sells 100% direct on negotiated terms, and earns **26.10%**. Xunjiexing has "over ten thousand" and
earns **8.52% with a net loss**. *(`PCB-5`)*

**TTM Technologies (NASDAQ: TTMI)** — twenty-five years of SEC filings say quick-turn earns a price

## 5. How to check this yourself

**The disclosure archive.** Chinese listing documents are published at **[cninfo 巨潮资讯网](http://www.cninfo.com.cn/)**,
the CSRC-designated disclosure site. Prospectuses are under `category=category_sf_szsh`. The search
endpoint is `http://www.cninfo.com.cn/new/hisAnnouncement/query` — **it is POST-only**, so pasting it
into a browser returns 404; use `curl -X POST` with form parameters. Shenzhen serves PDFs from
`reportdocs.static.szse.cn`; cninfo serves them from `static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/`.

**Link check, 2026-09-26.** Every one of the **19 Chinese filing PDFs linked above returned HTTP
200** when tested from a script. The **four `sec.gov` links return 403 to scripted requests** —
`www.sec.gov` blocks non-browser user agents — but they open normally in a browser. If you want them
programmatically, `data.sec.gov` is not blocked and serves the same filings' XBRL facts.

**Five cautions, in order of how likely they are to bite:**

1. **The English is ours, not the source's.** Every filing above is in Chinese. We quote the
   original alongside every translation *precisely so you can check it* — and you should.
2. **JLC's document is a 申报稿 — a filed *draft*.** It is the version submitted for review, not a
   completed listing. Figures can change in a later version.
3. **Don't quote 28.06% as the long-tail margin.** It is the blended whole-PCB figure and contains
   the 2.76% segment.
4. **These are PCB customers, not chip customers.** A JLC long-tail customer places orders averaging
   **US$67**. A specialty-fab tail customer averages about **€344,000** — a factor of ~5,000. We
   made this mistake ourselves and corrected it; see
   [`analyses/customers-needed-to-fill-a-fab.md`](analyses/customers-needed-to-fill-a-fab.md).
5. **Cost of acquisition is not in any of these filings.** Nothing here says what it costs JLC to
   acquire 1.36 million users. In commercial printing, where one company *does* publish below the
   gross line, a 23-point gross-margin lead becomes **0.26 points at segment EBITDA** — advertising
   eats **98.9%** of it (`OIM-6`). **That is the strongest unanswered objection to this whole file.**

## 6. The honest summary

**What the evidence supports.** Across **eighteen company-periods in five companies**, no
larger-order tier out-earns a smaller-order tier. All five decompositions reconcile to within
**0.01 pp** of the filings' own printed figures. Zhongfu is a controlled test — the same plant, layer
count held constant, small batch winning 8/8. Chongda states it as an industry property and gives
the mechanism. **This is not one company's marketing.**

**What it does not support.**

- **It is not general.** Two audited CDMOs print the same table upside down: 博腾股份 Porton's
  clinical/small-batch line ran at **6.84%** gross margin against **53.48%** for commercial volume;
  凯莱英 Asymchem 2024 was **39.42%** small against **53.32%** bulk (`OIM-3`, `OIM-4`). The
  long tail wins **where its orders are repetitions of a frozen process** — and loses where each
  small order is first-of-a-kind.
- **A long tail is not sufficient.** Xunjiexing has ten thousand customers and a net loss.
- **The advantage may not survive customer acquisition** — see caution 5.
- **It may be narrowing.** Where a time series exists, the premium is shrinking (`OIM-7`).
